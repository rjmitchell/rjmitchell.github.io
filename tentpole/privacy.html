Privacy policy

tentpole is a private app with a single user, its developer.

It reads that user's own health data from Google, read-only and with their consent, and uses it only to show them their own trends. The data is stored privately, is never sold or shared, and is not used for advertising.

Use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
